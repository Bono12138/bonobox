# Strategy case contract

Keep this internal decision record separate from the evidence that supports it. Do not force the user to read it unless they ask.

## Required content

- desired end condition and unacceptable downside;
- grounded facts, named assumptions and unanswered questions;
- minimum intermediate states between the current scene and the desired result;
- the first blocked transition and why the obvious route is blocked;
- available people, relationships, scenes, carriers, environmental levers and incentives;
- mechanism-distinct candidate routes;
- selected first move, actor, scene and timing;
- intended receiver, actual audience and transmission check;
- signal of progress, stopping condition, fallback and repair path;
- actual response and outcome after execution;
- failed assumptions and the next information to collect.

## Information boundaries

- A predicted reaction is a hypothesis, not a fact about a person.
- A strategy is not evidence that the strategy works.
- An observed reaction is case evidence within that scene.
- Sensitive actor profiles remain in the private case.
- A reusable pattern needs clear conditions and failures across different cases; one favourable story is not enough.
