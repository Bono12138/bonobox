# BonoBox Brand Guide

## Brand name

- Official name: **BonoBox**
- Short name: **BBX**
- Chinese nickname: **BB 箱子**
- Every official visual must contain the exact Latin word **Bono**. Do not rely on the mascot alone.
- Do not use previous program or column names in new public assets.

## Mascot

The BonoBox mascot is a cream-white, very long-eared rabbit wearing a slightly crooked bright-yellow construction helmet. It has round eyes with tiny pupils, a flat deadpan mouth, awkward stretched proportions, and a calm “I found another problem” expression.

Use rough hand-drawn closed black lines, simple flat fills, and dry visual humor. Keep the drawing intentionally imperfect. Do not turn it into a glossy corporate app icon, polished 3D mascot, or photorealistic animal.

The master logo shows the rabbit rising from a navy toolbox with a teal wrench. A bright-yellow label on the toolbox contains the exact word `Bono`.

## Visual system

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Warning yellow | `#F5C518` | Helmet, title highlight, fixed recognition anchor |
| Ink black | `#111111` | Hand-drawn outlines and primary text |
| Warm white | `#FFFDF7` | Rabbit and open background |
| Navy | `#0B3A67` | Toolbox, Wiki title area, repository structure |
| Teal | `#2B9A8F` | Small tool details and Wiki status accent |
| Red | `#D92D20` | Only for warnings, failure marks, or rejected reports |

Use yellow, black, and warm white as the character system. Navy and teal connect the mascot to the repository and Wiki. Red is functional, not decorative.

## Layout rules

- Keep the rabbit recognizable by its ears, eyes, helmet, and deadpan expression.
- Keep official titles short. Use hand-drawn bold lettering with slightly uneven edges.
- Make `Bono` visible on the main logo, repository hero, Wiki project card, and recurring program covers.
- Use the rabbit as a recurring corner anchor on content covers once its position is established.
- Do not add QR codes, social account names, secrets, company information, or personal photos to public repository assets.
- Check all generated text letter by letter before publication.
